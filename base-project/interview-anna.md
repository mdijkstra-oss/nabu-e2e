# Interview with Anna

Anna has worked remotely for six years as a graphic designer. She says the
freedom to set her own hours is the single biggest reason she stays. "I do my
best work before anyone else is awake," she told us, laughing.

She misses the office kitchen conversations. Ideas used to appear over coffee
without anyone scheduling them. Now every conversation needs a calendar
invite, and small questions feel too heavy to ask.

Her routine is strict: a walk before work, no email after six, and one office
day per month to keep faces attached to names.
