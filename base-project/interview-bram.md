# Interview with Bram

Bram is a support engineer who moved to a village after his company went
remote-first. Commuting used to cost him three hours a day; he now spends
that time with his children.

He worries about visibility. Promotions, he suspects, still go to the people
who are physically near the decision makers. "Out of sight is out of the
running," he said quietly.

His team fights isolation with a daily video standup and a shared chat
channel where nobody talks about work on Fridays.
